The Original Mycohobo

mycohobo (noun): A person who dedicates their life to observing and understanding fungi, living a nomadic lifestyle to follow mushroom fruitings.

Noah Siegel is a leading expert in US field mycology, with over three decades of experience in field identification, taxonomy, and photography — his ability to recognize and identify mushrooms is unparalleled. As a renowned field guide author, award-winning photographer, and sought-after speaker, Noah has dedicated his career to educating others about the fascinating world of fungi and contributing to the understanding and appreciation of fungi across the globe.

Noah has conducted field work across the United States, Canada, New Zealand, Australia, Cameroon, Guyana, and Tanzania. He is the author or co-author of three major field guides and has co-authored peer-reviewed publications in Mycologia, Fungal Systematics and Evolution, and other leading journals. His photographs have won multiple NAMA awards and been featured in numerous books and publications worldwide.

Books

Comprehensive field guides to the fungi of western North America.

Mushrooms of Cascadia

Noah Siegel & Christian Schwarz · Backcountry Press · 576 pages

Over 750 species with 800+ photographs. The definitive field guide to PNW fungi — from beginners to professionals. Covers delicious edibles and toxic look-alikes, rarities and regional specialties.

Available at speaking events and book tours

Mushrooms of Alaska

Steve Trudell, Noah Siegel & Kate Mohatt · Timber Press · 572 pages

The first comprehensive field guide to Alaska's fungi. Over 400 species with 500+ photographs, including rare species not covered in any other guide. The perfect companion for foragers and naturalists exploring America's wildest state.

Available at speaking events and book tours

Mushrooms of the Redwood Coast

Noah Siegel & Christian Schwarz · Ten Speed Press · 608 pages

The classic that started it all. Over 750 species of fungi found in the redwood and mixed-hardwood forests of coastal Northern California. The book that established Noah and Christian as the definitive voices in West Coast mycology.

Available at speaking events and book tours
