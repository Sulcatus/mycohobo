Talk Topics

Noah tailors presentations for audiences from curious beginners to professional mycologists. Popular talks include:

Mushrooms of Cascadia: A Fungal Paradise

Introduces listeners to the spectacular diversity of mushrooms in the PNW and how they grow across unique habitats.

Mushrooms of the Redwood Coast

Gain a better understanding about the mushrooms of northern California, hear some of the stories behind the book, and enjoy breathtaking photographs.

Exploring the Unknown: Cryptic Mushroom Diversity In Your Backyard

Modern taxonomical tools have shown that many fungi are undescribed. This talk highlights recent discoveries and how you can advance mycology in your area.

Money in the Bankeraceae: Taking a Bite Into Tooth Fungi

Bankeraceae — including Hydnellum, Sarcodon, Phellodon and Bankera — Noah explains the latest changes and tips on identification of this tricky group.

To Live and To Dye For

Edible, poisonous and dye mushrooms of Alaska — exploring the intersection of mycology, foraging, and traditional craft.

"Stop Walking on Truffles" — The Cryptic World Underground

Although truffles are renowned for their culinary properties, very few species are prized edibles. This talk highlights common species and how to find and identify them.
