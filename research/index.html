Field Surveys & Contract Work

Noah has extensive experience conducting biological surveys and mycological assessments for federal and state agencies, including the USDA Forest Service. His work includes:

  • Forest health and fungal diversity surveys across the Pacific Northwest
  • Mycological assessments for environmental impact studies
  • Species documentation and identification for conservation planning
  • Training and capacity building for field staff

Contact Noah for consulting inquiries and Forest Service bids.

Tanzania Chimp Mycophagy Project

Noah is contributing to groundbreaking research on fungal consumption by wild chimpanzees in the Issa Valley of western Tanzania, in collaboration with the Greater Mahale Ecosystem Research and Conservation (GMERC) project at University College London. This interdisciplinary project combines primatology and mycology to document the role of fungi in great ape diets.
