Comprehensive field guides to the fungi of western North America.

Mushrooms of Cascadia

Noah Siegel & Christian Schwarz · Backcountry Press · 576 pages · 2023

Mushrooms of Cascadia is a region-specific field guide co-authored by Noah Siegel, focusing on the diverse fungal species of the Pacific Northwest. The book provides in-depth, taxonomically accurate descriptions of over 750 species found in Cascadia's forests, with 800+ photographs, a special emphasis on identification keys, ecological roles, and habitats. Covers common and conspicuous species, delicious edibles and toxic look-alikes, rarities and regional specialties.

Available at speaking events and book tours

Mushrooms of Alaska

Steve Trudell, Noah Siegel & Kate Mohatt · Timber Press · 572 pages · 2025

The first comprehensive field guide to Alaska's fungi. From the leading experts on Northwest mycology — over 400 species described and illustrated with 500+ photographs, including rare species not covered in any other guide. Color-coded layout with identification keys make it accessible for beginners while offering the depth experts need. Covers the most conspicuous, distinctive, and ecologically important mushrooms found in the Last Frontier — the perfect companion for foragers, hikers, and naturalists exploring America's wildest state.

Available at speaking events and book tours

Mushrooms of the Redwood Coast

Noah Siegel & Christian Schwarz · Ten Speed Press · 608 pages · 2016

Mushrooms of the Redwood Coast is a comprehensive field guide designed for both amateur and expert mycologists. The book covers over 700 species of fungi native to Northern California's coastal regions. With detailed species descriptions, habitat information, and stunning full-color photographs, it serves as an invaluable resource for mushroom identification. The guide emphasizes ecology and taxonomy, providing in-depth insights into the rich fungal diversity of the redwood forest ecosystem. The classic that established Noah and Christian as the definitive voices in West Coast mycology.

Available at speaking events and book tours
